# The books

renders it — see [one workspace for every book](/decisions/one-workspace-for-books.md).

* [Rust for Failures](rust4failures.md) - the real book, and the project's in-flight Phase 5 migration. Two steps so far.

*Controllability: The Craft of Building Observable Systems* is named in the spec
as the second real book, and has not been started.
